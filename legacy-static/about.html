Who we are

More than a general store

Sri Sai Natural Foods is a general store on Kaman Main Road in Hafeezpet, Hyderabad, built around one simple idea: natural food, prepared and sold the way it should be.

The shelves carry a wide variety of wood-pressed and cold-pressed oils, fresh pulses and dals, ghee and raw food items — with a quality and freshness that regulars say stands out compared to other stores around.

What customers notice most is the care: mustard oil pressed on clean machines, sometimes right in front of them, and staff who take the time to explain the health benefits of every product at honest, reasonable prices.

Our specialty

Wood-pressed oils, pressed fresh

Our oil press machine is at the heart of what we do. Customers can come in and watch their 5-litre tin of mustard oil pressed right in front of them — on clean, well-maintained wooden machines.

This traditional kachi ghani method uses no heat, which means the oil retains its natural nutrients, aroma and pungency. What you get is pure, fresh oil — exactly as nature intended.

We stock wood-pressed mustard oil, groundnut oil, coconut oil, sesame oil, and sunflower oil — plus cold-pressed flaxseed oil for health-conscious families.
